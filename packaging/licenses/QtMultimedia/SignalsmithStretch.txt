Signalsmith Stretch, version 1.0.0 | Qt Multimedia | Qt 6.11.2
Source: https://doc.qt.io/qt-6.11/qtmultimedia-attribution-signalsmith-stretch.html

This is a C++11 header-only class for pitch-shifting and time-stretching.


Used in the FFMpeg media backend for pitch compensation (QMediaPlayer).


The sources can be found in qtmultimedia/src/3rdparty/signalsmith-stretch.
